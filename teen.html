GOOD TO KNOW

Before you start.

Is Funngro free to join? +

The current Funngro Teen page describes the service as free to join with no registration or joining fees.

How much can I earn? +

Project payouts vary by campaign, task and eligibility. Treat displayed examples as examples, not guaranteed income.

How old do I need to be? +

Eligibility can depend on the platform's current terms and the individual campaign. Check the current official Funngro terms before participating.

What should I do if a project is unclear? +

Read the brief and any eligibility requirements before starting. If something is unclear, use the platform's official support route rather than guessing.
