FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Funngro? +

Funngro connects young people with flexible projects from brands, including promotion, content, referrals, surveys and other tasks.

Do I need to pay to join? +

The current Funngro site describes the platform as free to join, with no registration or joining fee.

What kind of work can I find? +

Opportunities can include content creation, brand promotion, referrals, sampling, surveys and other micro tasks.

How do payouts work? +

Eligible completed projects are verified before payout. Funngro currently describes UPI and bank-transfer payouts.
